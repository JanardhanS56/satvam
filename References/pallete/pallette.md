**60% Dominant: Warm Ivory**

* **Hex:** #FAF7F0
* **Role:** The foundational canvas.
* **Usage:** Main website background, product card bases, and generous negative space. This exact shade ties directly into your product photography backgrounds, ensuring a seamless, editorial blend between the images and the site.

**30% Secondary: Deep Olive Green**

* **Hex:** #51902fff
* **Role:** Structure and readability.
* **Usage:** Primary typography (H1/H2 headings), navigation menus, footer background, and secondary button outlines. This grounds the layout and matches the deep green typography from your refined logo.

**10% Accent: Antique Saffron Gold**

* **Hex:** #C39B57
* **Role:** The conversion driver.
* **Usage:** Primary CTA buttons ("Add to Cart", "Checkout"), text links, hover states, and minimal icon highlights. This pulls from the golden filigree and lotus in your logo, drawing the eye directly to clickable actions without breaking the luxury apothecary aesthetic.

**Text & Contrast Guidelines:**

* **Body Text:** Use a very dark charcoal (like #1A1A1A) instead of pure black for body paragraphs to maintain that warm, printed-editorial feel against the ivory.
* **Reverse Text:** Use the ivory (#FAF7F0) for text that sits on top of the dark olive footer or buttons.
